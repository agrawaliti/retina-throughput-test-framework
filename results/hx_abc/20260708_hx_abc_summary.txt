  Method: single cluster, sequential A/B/C reconfigured in place.
    A = baseline (no Retina)

KEY OBSERVATIONS
  - Baseline HX ceiling is very high: ~19 Gbps at 1KB, ~75 Gbps at 8KB/64KB
    (HX accelerated networking + 175 cores). Much higher than D32s_v3.
  - With 14,000 flows on 175 cores the packet rate is enormous. BOTH Retina
    buffer modes collapse to ~1.5-2.3 Gbps regardless of payload: the in-kernel
    packetparser eBPF datapath cost dominates and throttles throughput ~90-97%.
  - Ring-buffer vs perf-array at this extreme pps: essentially a wash within
    noise (1024: ring 2.33 > perf 1.49; 8192: perf 2.33 > ring 2.00; 65536: ~tie).
    Neither mode keeps up with a 175-core sender's packet rate.
  - Contrast with 32-core D32s_v3 (2026-07-08): there Retina cost vanished at
    64KB. On HX the far higher pps makes per-packet cost dominant at ALL payloads.
